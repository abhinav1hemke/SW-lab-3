SRS EXCERPT: Simple Account Portal

REQ-01: A visitor can register with a unique email address and a password
containing at least eight characters. The system confirms successful
registration and rejects an email address that is already registered.

REQ-02: A registered user can sign in with their email address and password
and sign out. The system displays an error when the credentials are invalid.

REQ-03: A registered user can request a password reset using their registered
email address. The system confirms the request without revealing whether an
unregistered email address exists.
