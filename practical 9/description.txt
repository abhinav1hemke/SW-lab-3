LIBRARY ER DIAGRAM DESCRIPTION

Create an entity-relationship diagram for a small library lending system.

Entities and attributes:
- LibraryMember: member_id (primary key), name, email (unique).
- Book: isbn (primary key), title, author.
- Loan: loan_id (primary key), member_id (foreign key), isbn (foreign key),
  loan_date, due_date, return_date (optional).
- Reservation: reservation_id (primary key), member_id (foreign key), isbn
  (foreign key), reservation_date, status.

Relationships:
- One LibraryMember can have zero or many Loans; every Loan belongs to one
  LibraryMember.
- One Book can appear in zero or many Loans over time; every Loan refers to one
  Book.
- One LibraryMember can place zero or many Reservations; every Reservation
  belongs to one LibraryMember.
- One Book can have zero or many Reservations; every Reservation refers to
  one Book.

Use entity boxes with their key attributes and label the one-to-many
relationships clearly.
